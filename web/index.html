A universe with its optimizations exposed

Physical limits, on this framework's reading, are not fundamental truths but resource decisions — things a creator wrote to run a universe cheaply. Below is one running. Turn its limits off and watch what they were buying.

a computed cell a region held as one density the probe rendered collapsed

Grainy ground is being computed cell by cell. Smooth ground is not being computed at all — it is one number standing in for a whole region, and it only becomes cells when the probe arrives.

The four limits

Each is an optimization. Switch one off to buy a more expensive, more faithful universe.

What this shows: that these ideas are coherent — that they can be made to work together in something that runs and produces measurable results.

What it does not show: that our universe works this way. Every number here is a fact about a program. Nothing on this page is evidence about physics, and the model makes falsifiable predictions only about its own behaviour.
